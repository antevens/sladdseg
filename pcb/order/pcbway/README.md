# PCBWay order

Generated by `make order` (`pcb/tools/order_pcbway.py`): a devkit of each variant, 5 PCBs of every design (PCBWay's minimum; more where both variants use the board) and 5 sets of parts per variant (`make order DEVKIT_SETS=…`), in two options:

- **Option A** (`option_a_pcbway_smt/`): PCBWay makes the boards and fits the SMD parts (turnkey, they source the parts). You solder the THT parts and the rockers.
- **Option B** (`option_b_self_solder/`): PCBWay makes bare boards; you buy every part and solder everything.

Do the human reviews in `pcb/fab/README.md` and `pcb/eu/fab/README.md` (fuse rating, isolation, thermal, approvals) before ordering the mains boards. Nothing here is a compliance claim.

**Prototypes.** The ring board, the EU cap board and the back cover flex are not confirmed yet; their files are named `_PROTOTYPE` and are for fit tests only:

- ring_board: FIT-TEST PROTOTYPE: J2 is the Molex 55909-2474 plug for the MSR-2's CN2 (D-82) on Molex's land pattern (D-89), but its pin 1 and contact map are placeholders until Apollo's pinout is in hand: the MSR-2's signals will not line up. PCBWay does not fit J2.
- eu_cap_board: FIT-TEST PROTOTYPE: J2 is the Molex 55909-2474 plug for the MSR-2's CN2 (D-82) on Molex's land pattern (D-89), but its pin 1 and contact map are placeholders until Apollo's pinout is in hand: the MSR-2's signals will not line up. PCBWay does not fit J2.
- cover_flex: FIT-TEST PROTOTYPE: pad, contact and regulator positions come from photos and drawings (REF / MEASURE) until measured on a remote.

## Option A: PCB + SMD assembly by PCBWay

Per board in `option_a_pcbway_smt/<board>/`: the Gerber zip, `<board>_pcbway_bom.csv`, `<board>_pcbway_centroid.csv`, the assembly or fab drawings.

1. pcbway.com → PCB Assembly → Turnkey (the cover flex: Flex PCB with assembly). Upload the Gerber zip and fill the board settings from the table.
2. Assembly: SMT only, the sides the centroid lists; the quantity to assemble is the plate count (column "PCBs to order").
3. Upload the BOM and the centroid. The BOM lists only the SMD parts PCBWay fits; THT parts, the rockers and the prototypes' placeholder CN2 mates are not in it (do not fit). Safety-critical parts (F1, RV1) must be the listed MPN: no substitutes without review.
4. Add the board's notes above to the order remarks.
5. Buy the rest from DigiKey or Mouser: `parts_digikey.csv` / `parts_mouser.csv` (the lines marked NOT AT … go to the other distributor), imported as described in `pcb/order/README.md`. They hold the THT parts, the rockers and the parts not on a board (NA: studs, standoffs, FFC; EU: the PE lever connector).

## Option B: bare boards, you solder

Per board in `option_b_self_solder/<board>/`: the Gerber zip (the prototypes also their fab drawing).

1. pcbway.com → PCB Instant Quote (the cover flex: Flex PCB). Upload the Gerber zip, fill the board settings from the table.
2. Optional: add a stencil (framework-less, top) for the boards with SMD parts: the paste layers are in the Gerbers. The microphone (LGA), the SHT40 (DFN), the WA-SMSI posts and spacers and the P70 spring pins are reflow parts (hot plate or hot air with paste). The ADAU7002 (WLCSP-8, 0.4 mm pitch) is not a hand part: order the ring board and the EU cap board with PCBWay assembly (option A) even here.
3. Buy every part: `parts_digikey.csv` / `parts_mouser.csv` (the lines marked NOT AT … go to the other distributor).

## Quantities

Part quantities are exactly 5 sets per variant, no spares (`pcb/order/README.md`). PCBWay's minimum is 5 boards per design; the spare board costs little and covers a failed assembly.
