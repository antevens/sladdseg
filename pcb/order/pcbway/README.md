# PCBWay order

Generated by `make order` (`pcb/tools/order_pcbway.py`) for 3 NA and 2 EU test plates (`make order NA_PLATES=… EU_PLATES=…`). Two options for the same boards:

- **Option A** (`option_a_pcbway_smt/`): PCBWay makes the boards and fits the SMD parts (turnkey, they source the parts). You solder the THT parts and the rockers.
- **Option B** (`option_b_self_solder/`): PCBWay makes bare boards; you buy every part and solder everything.

Do the human reviews in `pcb/fab/README.md` and `pcb/eu/fab/README.md` (fuse rating, isolation, thermal, approvals) before ordering the mains boards. Nothing here is a compliance claim.

## Option A: PCB + SMD assembly by PCBWay

Per board in `option_a_pcbway_smt/<board>/`: `<board>_v…_gerbers.zip`, `<board>_pcbway_bom.csv`, `<board>_pcbway_centroid.csv`, assembly drawings.

1. pcbway.com → PCB Assembly → Turnkey. Upload the Gerber zip and fill the board settings from the table.
2. Assembly: SMT only, top side unless the centroid lists Bottom parts; the quantity to assemble is the plate count (column "PCBs to order").
3. Upload the BOM and the centroid. The BOM lists only the SMD parts PCBWay fits; THT parts and the rockers are not in it (do not fit). Safety-critical parts (F1, RV1) must be the listed MPN: no substitutes without review.
4. Add the board's notes above to the order remarks.
5. Buy the rest from DigiKey or Mouser: `parts_digikey.csv` / `parts_mouser.csv` (and the `_rest_` file for parts the other distributor does not carry), imported as described in `pcb/order/README.md`. They hold the THT parts, the rockers and the NA mechanical parts (studs, standoffs, FFC).

## Option B: bare boards, you solder

Per board in `option_b_self_solder/<board>/`: the Gerber zip.

1. pcbway.com → PCB Instant Quote. Upload the Gerber zip, fill the board settings from the table.
2. Optional: add a stencil (framework-less, top) for the boards with SMD parts: the paste layers are in the Gerbers. The mic carrier's bottom-port LGA microphone, the WA-SMSI posts and the P70 spring pins are reflow parts (hot plate or hot air with paste).
3. Buy every part: `parts_digikey.csv` / `parts_mouser.csv` (and the `_rest_` file).

## Quantities

Part quantities are per plate times the plates plus spares (rules in `pcb/order/README.md`). PCBWay's minimum is 5 boards per design; the spare boards cost little and cover a failed assembly.
