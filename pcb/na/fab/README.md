## PCB order settings

- Size 47 × 72 mm, 2 layers, standard FR-4, 1.6 mm.
- **2 oz** copper (sets the PE ring's rating, D-153), **HASL lead-free**. Solder mask and silkscreen on both sides;
  the bars' lands are copper with no solder mask (the mask openings are in the Gerbers).
- **The outline has a closed routed slot** under PS1, 2 mm wide with round ends, stopping 5.5 mm above the bottom
  edge. It is part of Edge.Cuts, not a drill. Add "routed isolation slot in outline, do not omit" to the order
  remarks.
- Four 3.2 mm mounting holes, all unplated (NPTH drill file), with no copper under the standoffs and screw heads.
- No castellations, impedance control or V-cut.

## Bars

- PCBWay CNC / laser cutting from `na_mains_board_busbar_v0.7_bars.dxf`: mm, one closed outline per bar with its pin
  holes; copper C110, **0.5 mm**, tin plated, four pieces per board (BAR1–BAR4 in the BOM, not fitted).

## Assembly

- Front (F): F1, RV1, J2, P1, P2 (SMD, reflow; P1 / P2 are Würth WA-SMSI M2 spacers on a Ø5.3 ring over a Ø3.0 unplated hole). Back (B): J1, PS1 (THT).
- **S1 and the bars are not fitted by PCBWay.** The bars go on first, front side, before the board goes into the
  barrier: each bar on its land, over its WAGO pin tails (hot plate or a large iron), solder filling the holes.
- The rocker snaps into the printed barrier from the front, then the board goes onto its pins from behind (they pass
  the bars' slots). **Solder S1 by hand only after the board is screwed down on its four standoffs**: its holes are
  5.2 mm plated slots (D-88), so the panel cutout, not the board, locates the rocker; its solder fills the plated
  slots up to the bars.
- PS1 sits flush on the back. IRM-03 soldering limits per its datasheet: wave 265 °C 5 s,
  hand 390 °C 3 s.
- J1 wire entries face the board's top edge (towards S1), away from PS1.
- Source exactly the BOM MPNs for S1, J1, PS1, F1 and RV1 (safety-critical; no substitutes
  without review).

## Human review before ordering

- [ ] F1 rating T1A is the designer's choice; Mean Well gives none (inrush 20 A typ at
      230 VAC). Its 200 A breaking capacity at 250 VAC needs review against the branch
      circuit's prospective fault current.
- [ ] Isolation targets in `../kicad/na_mains_board_busbar.kicad_dru` (D-153: any two mains nets ≥ 3.2 mm on standard
      FR4, IEC 60664-1 creepage at the 320 V row, PD2; mains to 5 V ≥ 8.0 mm plus the slot) reviewed against IEC
      60664-1 / 62368-1, UL/CSA 62368-1 and the switch standard (IEC 60669-1); L and N bars both on the front (hard
      constraint 1).
- [ ] Thermal review of the bars (narrowest 1.4 mm, 0.70 mm²) and of the PE ring (front band, back pour, 22 stitching
      vias) at 2 oz for the branch circuit's fault current (D-153).
- [ ] The rocker's pin stems over the bars: on a sample, whether the 1802.2504's stems reach the board at the pads (our
      model of its drawing says they do); the bars lift it by 0.5 mm plus solder.
- [ ] The room between the board's front and the barrier for the bars (not in the CAD yet).
- [ ] WAGO 2604 pin size against the bars' 1.3 mm holes (MEASURE on a part).
- [ ] J1 pin rows (8.2 mm apart, from WAGO's 3D model, D-37) checked on a part.
- [ ] S1 pin numbering checked with a meter on a sample (D-26).
- [ ] Second-source rocker E-Switch RA412C1100 (D-88): its tab rows on a sample, tabs soldered into the
      slots outside the maker's listed terminations (D-86), its 10(4) A ENEC rating accepted.
- [ ] Approvals of S1, J1, F1 and RV1 adequate for the BC homeowner permit and inspector.
- [ ] Gerbers opened in a viewer (PCBWay's online viewer is fine): the slot is present.
- [ ] Optional conformal coating after assembly (D-152, docs/ASSEMBLY.md): used or not, the coating chosen; not counted towards the spacing; over the bars' edges too, never into the rocker's slots.
